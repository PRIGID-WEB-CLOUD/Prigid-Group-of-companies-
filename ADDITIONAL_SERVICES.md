## 1. Infrastructure, Domain & Hosting Services

### 1.1 Custom Domain Registration & DNS Management
* **Service Code**: `ADDON-DOMAIN-REG`
* **Billing Model**: Annual Renewal
* **Description**: Complete registration, DNS record configuration, and renewal management for custom domains (`.com`, `.com.gh`, `.store`, `.shop`, `.org`, `.co`).
* **Deliverables**:
  - Domain acquisition and WHOIS privacy protection.
  - Automated Cloudflare DNS routing and DDoS mitigation.
  - Custom branded MX records configuration for professional business email (Google Workspace / Microsoft 365).

### 1.2 Managed Cloud Hosting & Infrastructure Operations
* **Service Code**: `ADDON-HOST-MGD`
* **Billing Model**: Monthly or Annual Retainer
* **Description**: Fully managed enterprise cloud hosting with high-availability architecture for clients operating Basic, Professional, or Custom tiers who prefer hands-off infrastructure management.
* **Deliverables**:
  - Managed Node.js / PostgreSQL cloud database hosting with 99.9% uptime SLA.
  - Daily automated encrypted database backups with point-in-time recovery.
  - Automated SSL/TLS wildcard certificate provisioning and auto-renewals.
  - Global CDN edge caching and image asset delivery optimization.

### 1.3 Post-Warranty Maintenance & Technical Retainer
* **Service Code**: `ADDON-MAINT-RETAINER`
* **Billing Model**: Monthly Retainer
* **Description**: Ongoing technical support, security patching, and emergency response following the expiration of the tier’s included support window (1 month for Basic, 3 months for Professional, 6 months for Premium).
* **Deliverables**:
  - Priority bug fixing and dependency security patch updates.
  - Monthly database vacuuming, performance audits, and index optimization.
  - Up to 5 hours of dedicated monthly developer support for minor tweaks, catalog updates, or promotional banners.

### Service Ordering & Commercial Engagement Process

1. **Selection & Quotation**: Client selects desired additional services during package selection or via written request to PRIGID.
2. **Statement of Work (SOW)**: PRIGID issues a standardized service addendum outlining deliverables, timeline, and fee structure.
3. **Execution & Delivery**: PRIGID’s engineering and creative teams deliver the services directly into the client's live instance.
4. **Sign-Off & Handover**: Client reviews and approves deliverables, followed by formal handover.
